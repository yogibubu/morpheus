The Journal of Chemical Physics}

\opening{Dear Editor,}

Please find enclosed the manuscript ``Algorithmic Construction and Exploitation
of Topology-Constrained Refinement Models for Semiexperimental Equilibrium
Structures,'' submitted as an Article to \emph{The Journal of Chemical
Physics}.

The manuscript introduces MORPHEUS, a framework that turns semiexperimental
refinement-model formulation into a reproducible topology-, symmetry-, and
rank-audited procedure. Starting from non-redundant SONIC coordinate contracts,
MORPHEUS generates the supported active space, applies constraints, predicates,
and parameter classes consistently, and propagates uncertainties to chemically
interpretable structural parameters.

The work addresses a general problem at the interface of rotational
spectroscopy, molecular structure determination, and quantum chemistry: how to
combine precise experimental data, computed vibrational and electronic
corrections, topology, symmetry, and external reference information in a
controlled inverse model. Validation spans acyclic, cyclic, planar, fused,
bridged, and flexible molecules. A 49-atom testosterone case further shows that
accurate equilibrium geometries, accelerated vibrational corrections, and
generated class-constrained refinement models can be chained when isotope-rich
semiexperimental analyses are not practical.

The source, runnable examples, benchmark outputs, and final-validation data are
distributed with the public MORPHEUS 0.1.0rc8 release. The Supporting
Information provides the numerical diagnostics and audit trails. The companion
SMITH/SONIC coordinate methodology is available as
\href{https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.16550}{arXiv:2607.16550}.

This manuscript is original, has not been published previously, and is not
under consideration elsewhere. I confirm that there are no conflicts of
interest to declare.

\closing{Sincerely,}
